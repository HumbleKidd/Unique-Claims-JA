Payment Methods

We accept direct bank transfers via NCB (National Commercial Bank). Cross-transfers from other Jamaican banks can take up to 2 business days to clear. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for in-person pickups.

Shipping & Collection

For out-of-parish and regional customers across Jamaica, our official shipping partner is KNUTSFORD EXPRESS. Bundling items optimizes your shipping cost.
